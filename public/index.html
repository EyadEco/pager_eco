Does the customer need an app or an account?

No account or app download is needed. The customer opens the order page in their browser from its QR code, or enters the order number.

What does the customer enter if they cannot scan?

Only the order number shown by the cashier. They do not need to enter a name, phone number, or other personal details.

Can this connect to my POS system?

The current project includes a standalone cashier dashboard. A POS connection needs to be built for the specific provider and account; the demo does not claim a live connection yet.

Will the alert make a sound?

The app requests an audible notification. The customer’s phone settings, notification permission, and focus or silent mode determine whether it plays a sound.

Do I need a tablet or pager device?

No dedicated pager hardware is needed. Staff can use the cashier dashboard on a computer, tablet, or phone.
